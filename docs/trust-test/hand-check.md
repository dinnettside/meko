# Hand-check of 30 feed claims

Done on 2026-10-20, against the source pages themselves, not against the quotes Meko saved. From each of the ten
feed videos, the three sentences with the most checkable detail (numbers, names and places) were picked by a script,
using the fact-checked wording the video was made with. Each source page was downloaded and turned into plain text;
where a site blocked automated access, an archived copy, the PDF or the Europe PMC API was used instead.

- Sentences: 25 of 30 supported by their pages, 5 partly, none contradicted.
- Quotes: 74 of 82 found word for word on their page or its archived copy. The other 8 could not be checked: 4 on Khan
  Academy pages that load their text with JavaScript and 4 on Britannica pages that block automated access.

"Quotes found" counts the quotes behind the sentence that were found word for word on their page.
